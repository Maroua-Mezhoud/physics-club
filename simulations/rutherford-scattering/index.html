THE PHYSICS

What should you observe?

  • Smaller impact parameters produce larger deflection angles.
  • Higher alpha-particle energy reduces the deflection.
  • A nucleus with a larger atomic number produces stronger repulsion.
  • Most particles with large impact parameters are deflected only slightly.

MODEL LIMITS

Interpret the results

This is a classical, educational model of Coulomb scattering. It treats the nucleus as a fixed point charge and does not include nuclear size, electron screening, or quantum effects. The animation is not a scale-accurate picture of an atom.
