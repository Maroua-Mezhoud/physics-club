# PHYSICS CLUB

**Computational Physics · Scientific Simulations · Interactive Exploration**

A university physics club project exploring the intersection of physics and programming through interactive scientific simulations and a developing digital platform.

🌐 **Live Website:** [Explore PHYSICS CLUB](https://maroua-mezhoud.github.io/physics-club/)

## Scientific Simulations

The project brings physical concepts to life through computational models and interactive visualizations.

### Quantum Harmonic Oscillator

An interactive exploration of the quantum harmonic oscillator, connecting quantum-mechanical eigenstates with their wavefunctions and probability distributions.

### Rutherford Scattering

A computational visualization of alpha-particle scattering, exploring how particle trajectories respond to electrostatic interactions and how scattering depends on physical parameters.

### Atomic Orbitals — In Development

A planned three-dimensional exploration of atomic orbitals, including the characteristic shapes associated with the s, p, and d subshells.

---

## A Developing Digital Platform

Beyond its scientific content, PHYSICS CLUB is developing the infrastructure needed to support a university physics community.

- **Interactive website** — a dedicated space for scientific projects and computational exploration.
- **Membership system** — an evolving application process for students interested in joining the club.
- **Administration dashboard** — an interface being developed to manage membership applications and club administration.
- **Database integration** — Supabase is used to support the platform's evolving data-management functionality.

Features and administrative workflows are being developed and refined as the project progresses.
